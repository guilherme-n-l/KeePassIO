## Description (4000)

KeePassIO is a free, open-source password manager for KeePass databases. Open the .kdbx file you already use with KeePassXC or KeePass, keep it wherever you like in the Files app, and use it on your iPhone with Face ID and AutoFill. There is no paywall, no account and no subscription.

YOUR DATABASE, YOUR STORAGE
• Opens and saves KDBX 4 databases, with a master password, a key file or both
• Keeps the file where you choose: on your iPhone, in iCloud Drive or in another app's folder. KeePassIO never uploads it anywhere itself
• If the file changed elsewhere, saving merges both versions instead of overwriting, and shows you what changed
• Create new databases and choose where they are saved; share a copy of the encrypted file whenever you like

FAST AND SECURE
• Unlock with Face ID or Touch ID, with a validity period you control
• Lock immediately or after a delay; changes are saved first
• Copied passwords stay on your device and clear themselves
• AutoFill passwords and one-time codes in Safari and other apps, and add a new entry right from the login form

FIND AND ORGANIZE
• Search that works like KeePassXC: words, fields, exclusions, wildcards, and paths such as mail/personal
• Drag entries and groups into other groups
• Entry and group icons, including the favicons stored by KeePassXC
• Attachments with preview and sharing
• Password history and a recycle bin

ONE-TIME CODES
• Add 2FA codes by scanning a QR code, choosing a QR image, pasting a link or typing the setup key
• Stored the KeePassXC way, so your other apps read them too

PASSWORD GENERATOR
• Passwords and passphrases with KeePassXC's options: length, character types, look-alike exclusion and custom characters

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts
• Doesn't connect to the internet unless you turn on website icons in Settings
• Open source under the MIT license: github.com/guilherme-n-l/KeePassIO

Works with databases from KeePass 2, KeePassXC and other apps that use the KDBX 4 format. Older KDBX 3 databases can be converted to KDBX 4 in KeePassXC.
